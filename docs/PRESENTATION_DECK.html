SYSTEM RESILIENCY
Critical Edge Cases & Architectural Defenses
09 / 10
Loss of Pay (LOP) & Prorations
Unapproved absences trigger automated LOP deduction: perDaySalary = gross / workingDays, paying strictly for effective worked days.
Cross-Midnight Shift Ingestion
Night shifts (22:00 → 06:00) lock check-out to the roster shift date rather than calendar date, avoiding broken duration calculations.
Missed Punch Regularization
Single-punch anomalies allow employees to raise an audit ticket. Approval retroactively re-evaluates working hours and resolves absences.
ThreadLocal Contamination Prevention
Tomcat pooled worker threads execute TenantContext.clear() in a mandatory finally block, guaranteeing zero cross-tenant leaks.
